How to play

  • Drive: ▲/W accelerate, ◀ ▶ or A/D steer, ▼/S brake. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons (the kart accelerates by itself).
  • ? boxes: drive through one and the race pauses for a question. Right earns an item. Wrong spins you out. ✕ or Esc skips with no penalty.
  • Items (Space or ★): 🔥 boost, 🛡️ shield (blocks your next spin-out), 🚀 rocket (slows the kart ahead). Rockets are more likely when you are behind.
  • Staying on the road matters: grass slows you and scenery stops you.
  • Topics: choose Economics, Accounting or English Language, Paper 1 or 2, and exact topics. Questions you get wrong come back more often.
  • Garage: spend coins on engine, turbo, tyres and nitro upgrades, and repaint your kart.
